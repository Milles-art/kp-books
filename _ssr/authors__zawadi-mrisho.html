Zawadi Mrisho

Zanzibar

Zawadi Mrisho writes about the coast: families, trade winds and the stubborn memory of the sea. She grew up between Stone Town and Tanga and now teaches creative writing.

Her novels move between English and Kiswahili the way her characters do.
